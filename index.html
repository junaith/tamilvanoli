தனியுரிமை கொள்கை — Privacy Policy

No Personal Data Collected

தமிழ் வானொலி does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. There are no user accounts, no login system, and no tracking infrastructure whatsoever.

Local Storage Only

Your saved (favourite) stations are stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared anytime via browser settings.

Third-Party API

Search queries are sent directly to radio-browser.info, an open-source public API. Their privacy policy governs those requests. We do not log or store your search terms.

Premium payments are processed entirely by Stripe Inc. We never see, store, or handle your card details. Stripe's Privacy Policy applies at stripe.com/privacy.

No Cookies · No Ads · No Analytics
